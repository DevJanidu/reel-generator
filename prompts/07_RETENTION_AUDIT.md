INPUT: approved script and provisional storyboard; optionally actual completed timeline and QC. OUTPUT: exactly ten Package retention entries with criterion, rating (strong/adequate/revise), concrete evidence and actionable recommendation. Criteria: Opening attention, Curiosity, Emotional stakes, Mid-story progression, Visual comprehensibility, Micro-payoffs, Ending satisfaction, Shareability, Originality, Brand consistency.

Describe the specific first-three-second event and central question, understandable character desire, causal developments, visual variety, earned payoff, fresh concept, Midnight Gold recognition, authentic reason to share and reason to return. State whether evidence is script-only, provisional storyboard or measured edit. Distinguish creative assumptions from verified scientific findings and actual audience data. Do not fabricate retention curves, viral odds or quantitative audience predictions.

Before approval, improve weak drafts internally. After approval, audit rather than rewriting. Critical contradictions block affected work; uncertain judgments become warnings. A technical export check cannot prove a narrative is emotionally satisfying. Completed edit audits include actual scene/caption coverage and known timing limitations; human review and listening remain useful.
