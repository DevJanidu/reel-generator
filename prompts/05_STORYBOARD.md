INPUT: approved script, character definitions and continuity. OUTPUT: scenes within Package schema. Assign consecutive stable S001-style IDs. narration_text spans partition the entire approved narration in order. A still must add action, reaction, clue, choice, obstacle, consequence, revelation or emotional development. Do not display a later outcome under earlier narration.

For each scene specify exact visible beat; cast IDs; full location and layout; props/counts/open/closed/damage states; expression and pose; primary target and story function; yellow surface and material versus emissive mechanism; real light source/direction; camera angle and portrait composition; continuity locks; normalized protected face/clue regions and caption-safe center position. Essential details should remain legible at mobile thumbnail size. No text is generated within artwork.

Before audio, calculate only provisional duration/coverage estimates. Do not claim exact spoken-word timestamps. After audio, preserve scene IDs and assets while grounding cuts in supplied subtitle cue boundaries; if a scene boundary lies inside an unaligned cue, require genuine corrected cue timing rather than distributing words evenly. Do not invent new events to fill time. No generated moving clips, image zoom/pan/parallax or animated character cutouts.

TRANSITIONS (ODD_FRAME_CINEMATIC_TRANSITIONS_V1): the stills stay completely static; only the boundary between two stills may be styled, and every transition is silent. For every scene choose transition_in, the transition FROM the previous still INTO this one, as an editor would, from exactly five names. hard_cut (the default, zero frames): visual hooks, sudden discoveries, reactions, changes of action, important beats, suspense, revelations and fast progression; it must feel intentional. graphic_match_cut (zero frames): only when the two neighbouring compositions are deliberately designed to correspond (same shape, position or framing, e.g. a round golden clock to a round golden eye); describe the match in both scenes' framing so the illustrations actually deliver it. short_crossfade (about 7 frames): gentle emotional continuity, quiet moments, friendship, sadness, romance, subtle atmospheric change; avoid it where two character silhouettes would overlap confusingly. emotional_crossfade (about 11 frames, sparingly): important emotional pauses, memories, reflection and gentle resolutions, never inside fast suspense or action. dip_to_charcoal (about 12 frames to #14171C and back, sparingly): meaningful time jumps, major location or chapter changes and dramatic separations, never an ordinary scene change. Explain the narrative purpose in transition_reason. S001 is always hard_cut. As a loose guide roughly 65% hard or graphic match cuts, 25% short crossfades and 10% dips or emotional crossfades, but these are not quotas: a tense mystery may be almost all hard cuts. Never choose at random, never transition every boundary, no wipes, slides, glitches, zooms or spins, and never invent other names: unknown names are rejected. Durations come from the pinned profile; transitions overlap existing scene boundaries and never change narration timing, caption timing or the total duration. Also mark at most one emphasis_phrase per scene (1-4 consecutive words from that scene's narration_text) for the key reveal, or leave it empty.
